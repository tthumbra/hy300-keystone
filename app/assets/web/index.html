Before you start

  1. Dim the room lights if you can.
  2. Stand where you can see the whole picture. Holding the phone sideways (landscape) usually fits it in more easily — either way works.
  3. Tap Start and allow the camera and motion access.

Wall direction (optional)

For the most accurate result, hold the back of your phone flat against the wall beside the picture, then tap Set. Then step back to where you'll aim from.

Calibrate

Keep the whole picture in view — small hand movements are fine. It measures, adjusts the projector, and checks again until the picture is rectangular. Tip: you can lean the phone against something facing the picture and let it run.

Fine-tune by hand

Each arrow moves that corner a little on the wall. Changes apply after a short pause. A ? means the page hasn't measured this projector's layout yet — run a calibration once and the arrows will be exact.
